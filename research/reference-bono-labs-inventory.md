## Comparison and adaptation boundaries

- Reuse the informational pattern of connecting services to their tools and showing what those tools can do for a business. BonnO Labs can also offer a clear progression from capabilities to project inquiry.
- Write BonnO Labs’ positioning, descriptions, examples, and interaction labels independently. Use its existing Netlify form as the inquiry destination.
- Keep visual identity, layout, copy, imagery, 3D choreography, and code original. Leave the reference’s project identities, contact details, location, history, statistics, estimates, ratings, and other business claims out of the public site.
- Keep portfolio entries explicitly conceptual until BonnO Labs supplies approved case studies. Keep unconfirmed company claims in offline drafts.
